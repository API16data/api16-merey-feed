How each record is built

1 · DetectedA large tanker leaves a Venezuelan crude terminal (José, Lake Maracaibo) or a Caribbean hub and its next port call is a U.S. refining port. Port visits come from Global Fishing Watch and API16's own AIS.
2 · EstimatedVolume comes from the ship's size and draught (±20%). Value uses the OPEC Merey price of the month it sailed. Each month is checked against EIA and U.S. customs totals.
3 · FingerprintedEach record gets a SHA-256 hash of its key fields, and the whole registry gets one too. If anyone changes a record, the fingerprint changes. This is the piece that will be written on-chain.

Evidence, not proof: an estimate is not a bill of lading. "High" means the voyage pattern is clear; "possible" means it fits but has gaps. Cases checked against official records live in the Cargo Ledger. Not financial advice.
