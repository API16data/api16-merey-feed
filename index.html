Four layers, shown in the bar above. Confirmed: official EIA monthly imports from Venezuela for completed months, then U.S. customs (Census) records for months EIA has not published yet; customs gives weight, converted at 152.5 kg per barrel of Merey 16, and the declared value; it can run below EIA because of entry timing. Preliminary: EIA weekly estimates after that, which EIA revises later. Estimated: the latest 4-week EIA rate applied to the days since the last weekly report, replaced by official data as it is published. Counts barrels that reached the U.S., not cargoes still at sea. Dollar value uses the OPEC Merey monthly average, and the declared customs value where customs data is used. Tankers from free AIS, refreshed every hour.
