Thousand barrels per day

Line: EIA weekly. Bars: EIA monthly by refinery. Squares: monthly exports to the US reported from ship tracking. Amber band: API16's AIS estimate.

  • EIA weekly: preliminary imports from the Weekly Petroleum Status Report, about 1 week behind. Revised later.
  • EIA monthly: final company-level data (who imported, where), about 2 months behind.
  • Reported: monthly exports to the US from ship-tracking data and PDVSA documents, as reported in the press.
  • API16 AIS estimate: tankers seen in Venezuelan or ABC waters that later reach a US Gulf port, times a cargo size estimated from the ship's length. Free AIS misses many ships near Venezuela, so this is a minimum.

Tankers on AIS

Tankers seen in the latest AIS scan (every hour, 10 minutes of listening). Amber = linked to Venezuela: seen in Venezuelan or ABC-island waters, or declaring a Venezuelan port as destination. Links are probable, not confirmed. Free AIS comes from land receivers, so coverage at Venezuelan terminals is thin; the US Gulf Coast is well covered.

Merey vs Brent

Merey 16 is Venezuela's reference crude: heavy (16° API) and sour. It does not trade on any exchange, so its price is published monthly. The gap to Brent is the "Venezuela discount": quality, freight and risk.

    On-chain Merey feed

    API16 publishes the monthly Merey and Brent averages to smart contracts with the same read interface as a Chainlink price feed, so any app can read them. Each month stores its period, whether the figure was reported or derived, and the source URL. Status: draft, tested on a local network, Base Sepolia testnet deployment next. Not audited.
